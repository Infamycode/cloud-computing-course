# Mission Reflection

This mission helped me understand that choosing a cloud platform is not only about choosing the most popular provider. The platform should be selected based on the actual needs of the business. Among AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, AWS impressed me the most because it offers a wide range of services and can support different types of organizations. I also liked that its services can scale as a company's requirements grow.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, identity management, security services, and tools for developing and deploying applications. They also provide global infrastructure and allow businesses to scale their resources without having to maintain all of the physical hardware themselves.

Business requirements are very important when selecting a cloud provider. For example, Azure is a good choice for a university that already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory because of its strong Microsoft integration. Google Cloud is also a strong option for AI and machine learning workloads, while AWS can be a good choice for startups and global web applications. This showed me that there is no single cloud provider that is best for every company.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to look at the problem from the client's point of view instead of simply choosing a platform based on personal preference. I learned how to compare services, identify the strengths of different providers, and match technology to business requirements.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved during this mission. I added organized research, comparison tables, recommendations, Linux investigation results, and screenshots as evidence. This makes the portfolio more useful as a record of the cloud computing skills and knowledge I have developed.
